If you want to play offline in your browser:

1. Click "index.html"
2. Click the download icon in the top right of the file

3. Double click "index.html" in your Finder


If you want to play via the launcher/dock:

1. Click "Tag.app.zip"
2. Click the download icon in the top right of the file

<img width="288" height="128" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-16 at 9 07 52 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2d9014ce-675c-4b5c-8d7f-a812f94179cb"

3. Unzip the app by double clicking it in the Finder
4. Cmd + Space, search "Terminal"
2. Open Terminal and type "xattr -cr ", don't press enter yet
3. REMEMBER THE SPACE AFTER -cr!
4. Drag the "Tag" application into the terminal
5. Press enter
6. Drag the "Tag" application anywhere into your dock
7. Click "Tag" from your dock or do cmd + space "Tag" whenever you want to play
